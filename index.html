    What is a QR Code?

    A QR (Quick Response) code is a two-dimensional matrix barcode invented in 1994 by Masahiro Hara at Denso Wave. While standard 1D retail barcodes hold roughly 20 numeric characters, a single QR code can encode up to 4,296 alphanumeric characters or 7,089 numeric digits along with built-in Reed-Solomon error correction.

    • Web Links & Menus: Direct mobile access to URLs, product manuals, and digital restaurant menus.
    • Payments & Tickets: Instant contactless payments (UPI, Alipay, PIX) and digital boarding passes.
    • Wi-Fi & Contact Sharing: Passwordless Wi-Fi login via WIFI: payloads and instant address book imports via vCards.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I scan a QR code without downloading an app?

    Open QR Vision in any mobile or desktop browser, click Start Camera Scan, allow camera permissions, and point your camera at the QR code. The decoded result appears immediately with zero app installation.

    Can I scan a QR code from a screenshot or saved image?

    Yes. Click Upload Image, drag and drop a PNG, JPG, or WEBP file into the drop zone, or press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac) to paste a screenshot directly from your clipboard.

    Is it safe and private to scan QR codes online?

    Yes. QR Vision processes every scan locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Your camera feed and image files never leave your device, and scan history is saved only in your own browser storage.

    Does QR Vision work on PC, Mac, iPhone, and Android?

    Yes. QR Vision works across Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS (Safari/Chrome), and Android devices using a built-in webcam, rear camera, or image files.

    What types of barcodes and QR payloads are supported?

    QR Vision parses URLs, Wi-Fi networks, vCard contacts, emails, phone numbers, SMS links, GPS coordinates, and plain text. For 1D product barcodes (EAN-13, UPC, Code 128, Code 39), use our Barcode Scanner.

    Is QR Vision completely free and available offline?

    Yes. QR Vision is 100% free with no limits or account sign-ups, and supports installation as a Progressive Web App (PWA) for offline use.
