    What is a QR Code?

    A QR (Quick Response) code is a two-dimensional matrix barcode invented in 1994 by Masahiro Hara at Denso Wave. Unlike traditional 1D barcodes limited to roughly 20 characters, a QR code stores up to 4,296 alphanumeric characters or 7,089 numeric digits with built-in Reed-Solomon error correction.

    • Web Links & Digital Menus: Instant mobile navigation without typing URLs.
    • Contactless Payments & Tickets: Supports UPI, PIX, boarding passes, and event entry.
    • Wi-Fi & Contact Cards: Encodes WIFI: credentials and vCard business cards for one-tap setup.
