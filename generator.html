Frequently Asked Questions

Do QR codes created with QR Vision expire?

No. QR Vision generates static QR codes directly in your browser. Your data is stored inside the QR pattern itself, so codes never expire and have unlimited scans.

How do I create a Wi-Fi QR code?

Choose Wi-Fi Network, type your network name (SSID), password, and encryption type, then download the PNG. Guests can scan it to connect automatically.
