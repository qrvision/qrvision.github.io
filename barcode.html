    Code 128 & Code 39

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Which barcode formats can QR Vision decode?

    QR Vision supports EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, Code 128, and Code 39 barcodes from both webcam streams and uploaded image files.

    Can I scan a barcode from an image or photo?

    Yes. Click Upload Barcode Image, drag and drop a photo, or paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V to decode the barcode instantly.

    Are my scanned barcodes uploaded to any server?

    No. All image analysis happens locally on your device using client-side JavaScript.
