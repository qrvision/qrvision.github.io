About QR Vision

QR Vision is a free, open-access web utility built to make scanning and generating QR codes and barcodes fast, accessible, and completely private across every device.

Our Privacy-First Architecture

Unlike traditional scanner apps that demand invasive permissions or upload images to remote servers, QR Vision performs 100% of image decoding and QR generation locally inside your web browser using modern HTML5 Canvas, WebRTC camera streams, and client-side JavaScript.

  • Zero server uploads — your camera feed and uploaded screenshots never leave your device.
  • Zero user accounts — open the site and scan immediately.
  • Offline capable — installable as a Progressive Web App (PWA) for scanning even without an active connection.

Available Tools

  • Online QR Code Scanner — Scan via camera, image upload, drag-and-drop, or clipboard paste.
  • Online Barcode Scanner — Decode EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, Code 128, and Code 39 barcodes.
  • Free QR Code Generator — Create static, non-expiring QR codes for URLs, Wi-Fi, text, email, and phone numbers.
