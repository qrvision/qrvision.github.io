Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

1. Zero Server Processing of Scanned Content

QR Vision is a static client-side web application. When you scan a QR code or barcode using your camera, upload an image file, or paste a screenshot, all decoding takes place strictly inside your browser. Neither your camera frames, images, nor decoded contents are ever transmitted to our servers or third parties.

2. Local Scan History

For your convenience, QR Vision stores up to your 30 most recent scans in your browser's local storage (localStorage). This data remains exclusively on your device and can be deleted at any time using the Clear History button.

3. Camera Permissions

Camera access is requested solely when you click Start Camera Scan. You can revoke camera permissions at any time through your browser settings.

4. Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) solely to measure aggregate traffic, page visits, and performance metrics. Google Analytics does not receive the contents of your scanned QR codes or barcodes.

5. Contact

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, please email hashtagfor@outlook.com.
